Before your tour

Important Tour Information

Weather and river conditions

Your safety is our priority. If weather or river conditions are unsuitable, WaiLusi may cancel or reschedule. If WaiLusi cancels for safety reasons, you may choose to reschedule or receive a full refund.

Cancellations and payment

Please contact WaiLusi as early as possible if you need to cancel or change your request. No online payment or deposit is taken. Payment is made in person on the day of the tour.

Late arrivals

Please arrive 15 minutes before departure. If you are running late, contact WaiLusi as soon as possible so the guide can arrange to wait for you.

Safety and children

All guests must follow the guide's safety instructions and wear required safety equipment. Children are welcome but must be accompanied by a parent or responsible adult.

Booking confirmation

A booking request is not automatically confirmed. Your tour is confirmed only after WaiLusi has agreed on the date, time and group size.
