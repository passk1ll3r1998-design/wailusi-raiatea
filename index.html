THE STORY BEHIND WAILUSI

A Natural Connection to Raiatea

Ia orana! Welcome to WaiLusi Raiatea. WaiLusi was created to share the natural beauty, culture and traditions of our beautiful island.

Our main focus is the beautiful Faaroa River, where visitors can discover the tropical environment through guided kayaking and local knowledge.

We believe that a great excursion is not only about the destination. It is also about the stories, the people, the nature and the memories you take home.

Join us and discover Raiatea naturally, one paddle at a time.
